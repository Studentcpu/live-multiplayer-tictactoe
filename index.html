How to play
  1. Two players share one room. The host is X; the second player is O.
  2. Players take turns placing their symbol in one empty square.
  3. First to get three symbols in a row, column, or diagonal wins.
  4. If all 9 squares are filled and nobody has three in a row, the game is a draw.
  5. A move is valid only on your turn and only in an empty square.
